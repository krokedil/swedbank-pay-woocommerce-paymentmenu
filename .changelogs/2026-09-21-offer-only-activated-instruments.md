Type: Feature
Needs Documentation: no

Separate instruments are now limited to the ones actually activated on the Swedbank Pay account. Instruments not activated are shown as locked in the settings with an explanation, are no longer offered at checkout, and an admin notice flags any instrument that is enabled but no longer activated. Instruments the account reports that the plugin does not list yet are offered too, labelled with their Swedbank Pay name. The account's activated instruments are refreshed on settings save and once daily. If the check can't be performed, every instrument is offered as before.
